WHICH CIRCUIT WROTE WHAT

      
agreedRatehire — the employer seals a salary commitment. The value shown is a hash; the rate and salt that open it never touch the chain.

      
active → trueacceptHire — the worker proves, in zero knowledge, that they hold the exact rate and salt behind their commitment.

      
approvedHoursapproveHours — public on purpose: both parties already know the hours. The row key is a hash of worker + period.

      
paidForconfirmPayment — the worker proves amount received = hours × sealed rate without revealing any of the three numbers.

      
contributionOkproveContribution — proves the social-security declaration was computed on real earnings, revealing neither.

      
active → falseendEmployment — the employer ends the relationship; the payment history stays verifiable forever.
